# Legal sources — privacy policy and booking terms

Every figure, deadline and percentage in `/legal/privacy-policy` and
`/legal/terms-and-conditions` was checked against the primary text rather than
written from memory. This table is here so a lawyer reviewing the pages can
confirm each one in minutes instead of hunting for it.

**This is not legal advice.** It is a citation trail for someone qualified to
check. Verified 6 September 2026.

## Package travel — Directive (EU) 2015/2302

Primary text: [EUR-Lex CELEX:32015L2302](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32015L2302)

| Terms § | Provision | Article | Verified text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | Package definition, organiser status | Art. 3(2) | Combination of at least two different types of travel service for the same trip |
| 2.1 | Standard information form before booking | Art. 5(1), Annex I | Form must be given "before the traveller is bound by any package travel contract or any corresponding offer"; Part A where hyperlinks are possible, Part B otherwise |
| 4 | Price increase cap | Art. 10(2) | Termination right where the increase exceeds "8 % of the total price of the package" |
| 4 | Notice period for a price increase | Art. 10(3) | "at the latest 20 days before the start of the package" |
| 4 | Price reduction, deduction of admin expenses | Art. 10(4)–(5) | Corresponding right to a decrease; organiser may deduct actual administrative expenses |
| 5.1 | Transferring the booking | Art. 9(1) | "Notice given at the latest seven days before the start of the package shall in any event be deemed to be reasonable" |
| 6.2 | Free termination for extraordinary circumstances | Art. 12(2) | Unavoidable and extraordinary circumstances at or near the destination significantly affecting performance; full refund, no additional compensation |
| 6.3 | Off-premises withdrawal right | Art. 12(5) | Member States *may* provide 14 days' withdrawal "without giving any reason" for off-premises contracts |
| 6.4 | Refund deadline | Art. 11(5), 12(4) | "without undue delay and in any event not later than 14 days after the package travel contract is terminated" |
| 7 | Minimum-numbers cancellation notice | Art. 12(3)(a) | 20 days for trips over 6 days; 7 days for trips of 2–6 days; 48 hours for trips under 2 days |
| 8 | Liability for performance | Art. 13(1) | Organiser is liable for performance of all travel services in the package |
| 8 | Price reduction and compensation | Art. 14(1)–(3) | Price reduction for any period of non-conformity; compensation without undue delay, with the three stated exceptions |
| 8 | Accommodation if return is prevented | Art. 13(7) | "for a period not exceeding three nights per traveller", with the stated exceptions for reduced mobility, pregnancy, unaccompanied minors and medical need |
| 8 | Assistance in difficulty | Art. 16 | Information on health services, local authorities, consular assistance; reasonable fee where the difficulty is caused intentionally or negligently by the traveller |
| 8 | Liability cap floor | Art. 14(4) | Any contractual limitation "does not amount to less than three times the total price of the package", and cannot cover personal injury or damage caused intentionally or negligently |
| 9 | Insolvency protection | Art. 17 | Security for refunds and, where carriage is included, repatriation |

## Consumer distance selling — Directive 2011/83/EU

Primary text: [EUR-Lex CELEX:32011L0083](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32011L0083)

| Terms § | Provision | Article | Verified text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 6.3 | Packages outside scope | Art. 3(3)(g) | Package travel contracts are excluded from the Directive's application |
| 3.5 | Payment surcharges | Art. 19 | Applies to packages by virtue of PTD Art. 27(2) |
| 3.5 | Basic-rate phone lines | Art. 21 | Applies to packages |
| 3.5 | No pre-ticked boxes, express consent for extras | Art. 22 | Applies to packages |

**Correction made during this check.** An earlier draft justified "no
cooling-off period" by the Article 16(l) exception for leisure services on a
specific date. The outcome is the same but the reasoning was wrong: packages
are excluded from the Directive's scope entirely under Article 3(3)(g), so
Article 16(l) never comes into play. The clause now cites the scope exclusion.
The same check surfaced Articles 19, 21 and 22, which PTD Art. 27(2) keeps
applying to packages — those were missing and have been added as § 3.5.

## What remains unverifiable from the outside

Three obligations are flagged in red on the pages themselves because no amount
of checking sources resolves them — they need a purchase, an appointment or a
document:

1. **Insolvency protection** (PTD Art. 17 / UK PTR reg. 19). Criminal offence
   in the UK if absent. Nothing on the site can substitute for the bond.
2. **EU representative** (GDPR Art. 27). An appointment, and the Art. 27(2)
   exemption needs assessing on the facts.
3. **Standard information form** (PTD Art. 5, Annex I). A document to produce
   and attach to the booking flow.

Two further points are jurisdiction-specific and were not researched per
member state, because the answer depends on where sales actually concentrate:

- Whether any member state where Courtside sells requires the terms to be
  available in its own language. The pages are English-only today, with a
  statement that English governs.
- Whether the termination fee scale in § 6.1 is defensible as "reasonable" in
  each market. The Directive permits standardised fees but they must reflect
  expected cost savings and resale income; § 6.1 offers a justification on
  request, which is the mechanism the Directive contemplates.
